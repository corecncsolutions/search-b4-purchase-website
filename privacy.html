Privacy Policy

Pre-launch notice: Search B4 Purchase is under development. This policy describes the intended operation and will be updated before public release to reflect the final app and service providers.

1. Who we are

Search B4 Purchase is a product of Core CNC Solutions Inc., an Ontario corporation located in Canada. References to “we,” “us” and “our” mean Core CNC Solutions Inc. in relation to Search B4 Purchase.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect account information, contact details, search queries, product photos or descriptions you submit, approximate or precise location when you grant permission, device and diagnostic information, subscription status and communications you send to support.

3. How we use information

  • Provide product search, comparison and research results.
  • Find relevant nearby or regional retailer information.
  • Operate accounts, subscriptions and free-search allowances.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot problems.
  • Improve the app and communicate about support or important service changes.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Photos, searches and location

If you submit a product photo, it may be processed to identify the product and generate research results. Location access is optional and will be requested through your device. You may deny or later disable location permission, although local results may become less accurate.

5. Service providers and external sources

We may use technology providers for hosting, analytics, product search, artificial intelligence, payment processing and customer support. Search results may include links to third-party retailers, manufacturers and information sources. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

6. Affiliate links

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. We may receive a commission if you complete a qualifying purchase, at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change the price you pay.

7. Retention and security

We intend to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with you. You may also control camera and location permissions using your device settings. See our Delete Account & Data page for instructions.

9. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. International processing

Some providers may process information outside Canada. When this occurs, information may be subject to the laws of the jurisdiction where it is processed.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.
