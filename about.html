Our purpose

Better information for better buying decisions.

About Search B4 Purchase

Search B4 Purchase is being created to make product research easier, clearer and more useful. Instead of checking multiple websites for scattered information, users will be able to review key buying details in one place.

Our goal is to help shoppers compare more than the advertised price. Product reliability, common faults, repairability, parts availability, manuals, warranty information and fair market value can all affect whether a purchase is truly worthwhile.

Built in Canada

Search B4 Purchase is a product of Core CNC Solutions Inc., an Ontario corporation. The service is planned to launch in Canada first, followed by expansion into the United States.

Our principles

  • Present useful information clearly.
  • Identify and link to relevant sources where possible.
  • Separate factual product research from sponsored or affiliate relationships.
  • Help users make their own informed purchasing decisions.
